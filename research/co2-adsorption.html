Research topic 01

CO₂ Adsorption in nanostructured materials

We study CO₂ adsorption in nanostructured materials, combining molecular simulation with collaborating research on supramolecular materials for gas separation and CO₂ storage.

Research focus

CoReLab investigates CO₂ adsorption, with Bahar Khanshaghaghi working on carbon nanostructures. Enrico Riccardi’s research includes molecular dynamics, rare-event simulation of adsorption, and multiscale modeling.

Collaborating research

Eva Rauls’s group studies molecules on surfaces using first-principles and semi-empirical calculations. Its collaboration with chemistry researchers connects computational studies of supramolecular and hybrid materials to synthesis and characterization for gas separation and CO₂ storage.

Related studies examine how molecular cavity size and functional groups influence CO₂ adsorption in pillararenes. Konstantinos Alektoridis and Eva Rauls also investigate functionalized dynarenes.
