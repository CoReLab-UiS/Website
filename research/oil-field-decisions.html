Research topic 03

Decision making analysis in oil field management

We combine reservoir flow modeling and decision analysis to study oil-field operations, with an emphasis on energy efficiency and decisions informed by evolving models and data.

Research focus

Our work brings together reservoir flow modeling, data assimilation and decision analysis. The group studies how models and new information can support decisions about oil-field development and operation.

Flow and energy use

Shipra Singh’s work uses streamline-based modeling to study fluid flow and travel time between injection and production wells. Her research also examines how perforation depth affects reservoir flow and injection energy.

Decision analysis

Enrico Riccardi’s research includes energy efficiency and multi-objective decision-making. Within CoReLab, David-Ioan Chitan works on oil-well decision analysis, while Auref Rostamian works on data assimilation and oil and gas field development.
