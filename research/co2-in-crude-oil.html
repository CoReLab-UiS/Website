Research topic 04

Molecular modeling of CO₂ dynamics in crude oil

This project studies CO₂ dynamics in crude oil through molecular modeling, with a focus on CO₂ storage in oil.

Research focus

Sunday Macdonald works on CO₂ storage in oil within Enrico Riccardi’s group. The project focuses on molecular modeling of CO₂ dynamics in crude oil.

For details about the current project and opportunities to participate, please contact the group.
